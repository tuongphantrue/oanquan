Each small pebble is one ordinary stone. Each QUAN piece is worth 10 points. A more natural hand now goes to each cell, picks up stones, holds them, and drops them one by one. Each dropped stone waits 2 seconds so you can follow the sowing clearly.

How to Play

You control the five BOTTOM pits. The computer controls the five TOP pits.

  1. Tap one of your non-empty pits.
  2. Choose CLOCKWISE or COUNTER-CLOCKWISE.
  3. The pebbles are distributed one-by-one around the board.
  4. If the next small pit still has pebbles, those pebbles are picked up and sowing continues.
  5. If sowing stops before an empty pit, you capture the stones in the following pit. Repeated captures are possible.
  6. The game ends after both Quan pieces are captured. Remaining stones on each player's side go to that player.

If all five pits on a player's side are empty at the start of a turn, five stones are placed back on that side and 5 points are deducted.

This is a simple common-rule variant designed for E-Ink play.
