Each small pebble is one ordinary stone. Each QUAN piece is worth 10 points.

How to Play

You control the five BOTTOM pits. The computer controls the five TOP pits.

  1. Tap one of your non-empty pits.
  2. Choose CLOCKWISE or COUNTER-CLOCKWISE.
  3. All stones from that pit are sown one-by-one around the board.
  4. If sowing ends before a non-empty small pit, take those stones and continue sowing in the same direction.
  5. If sowing ends before an empty pit, capture the stones (and Mandarin, if present) in the pit immediately after that empty pit. Repeated captures are possible.
  6. The game ends after both Mandarin pieces are captured. Remaining stones on each player's side go to that player.

If all five pits on a player's side are empty at the start of a turn, five stones are placed back on that side and 5 points are deducted.

This is a simple common-rule variant designed for E-Ink play.

Grayscale. Tap-only. Pebble display instead of numbers.
